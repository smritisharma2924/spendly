Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects and how it is used, stored, and protected when you use our personal expense tracking service.

Data We Collect

We collect the account information you provide, such as your name and email address, along with the expense details you choose to record, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information to provide your account, save and organize your expense records, display spending summaries, maintain the security of Spendly, and improve the service. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage

Your information is stored for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Spendly and meet applicable legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for services such as hosting, analytics, or account communications. These providers may process limited information only as needed to perform their services and are subject to their own privacy practices.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us using the support details made available through Spendly.
